<objective>
Wait out the clock, watch the app wake, and close the phase against evidence rather than assertion.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 5, as amended in 06-07, asks for a property with a **wall-clock
dependency that cannot complete inside one execution session**. Community Cloud sleeps an app after
exactly 12 hours without traffic and does not auto-wake it — a visitor lands on a sleep page and
clicks through. The verified property is therefore not "it is still up", which would be a lie about
the free tier, but "a logged-out visitor can wake it in one click and it then loads without error".
Only a human, at least 12 hours after the last traffic, can observe that.

This plan does not schedule work behind that wait. Everything else in the phase is already done, and
the only thing that follows the observation is the close-out.

The close-out itself is the second half. Every prior phase in this project discharged its criteria
against **named** evidence — a pytest node ID, a committed artifact, a recorded approval — rather
than against a claim. Phase 6's evidence is unusually mixed: some criteria rest on tests, one rests
on a build log a human read, one rests on a human waking a sleeping app. All of it gets named.

Output: a recorded cold-start observation, five criteria discharged against named evidence, two
requirements marked complete, and the phase closed.
</objective>

  <what-built>
The app is deployed and was confirmed live and usable from a logged-out browser at the end of plan
06-08, with its build log inspected and free of the four banned analysis packages, and its link is in
the README with a test behind it.

What is not yet observed is the property criterion 5 was actually protecting. Community Cloud's free
tier sleeps an app after exactly 12 hours without traffic and does not auto-wake it. Nothing about a
sleeping deployment is verifiable from the repository, and the wait cannot complete inside one
execution session — which is why this is a checkpoint with a wall-clock dependency and why no other
work is scheduled behind it.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
1. Note the timestamp of the last traffic recorded in `06-08-SUMMARY.md`. Do **not** open the app
   again until at least 12 hours after it — every visit resets the clock.
2. After 12+ hours, open the live URL in a **logged-out** browser: a fresh private/incognito window,
   not signed in to Streamlit, ideally on a different device or network from the one used to deploy.
3. Record what the **first paint** shows. Two outcomes are both acceptable and both must be reported
   as observed rather than assumed:
   - the app loaded directly (it had not slept, or was woken by other traffic), or
   - a sleep page appeared. If so, click through it, and confirm the app then loads without error.
4. Either way, once the app is up: confirm the sidebar is expanded, move the depth control at least
   once and confirm the headline numbers change, and confirm the three figures render.
5. Report back with: the elapsed time since last traffic, what the first paint showed, whether a
   click-through was required, roughly how long the wake took, and whether the app then worked.

If the app fails to load at all, or loads with an error, that is a real defect and this checkpoint is
not approved — report the error text rather than retrying until it works.
  </how-to-verify>

    - The reviewer's response is recorded verbatim in the plan summary
