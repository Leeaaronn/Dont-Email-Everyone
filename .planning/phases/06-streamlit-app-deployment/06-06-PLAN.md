section named `Assumptions, not data`, because a `k*` of 80% on first paint competing with a headline
depth of 20% is the misreading Pitfall 5 names.

**The footer and the caption rule.** D-09's literal reading: every displayed number gets a
one-line plain-language caption, with the data vintage and the outcome window in the footer.
