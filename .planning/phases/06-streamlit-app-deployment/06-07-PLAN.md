<objective>
Say out loud what this phase delivers, and have a human look at the thing no test can judge.

Purpose, first half: two clauses of ROADMAP criterion 1 and one clause of criterion 5 describe
behaviour this phase cannot and must not produce. "Versus emailing everyone" was replaced
project-wide by D-08a — its interval excludes zero from above at **0 of 909** band rows, so an app
headlining it would display a negative figure as the result. "Per treatment arm" and an arm selector
are forbidden by D-03 and D-04, and the artifact carries no mens ranking at all. Criterion 5's
"opens successfully from a logged-out browser after 12+ hours of no traffic" describes behaviour the
free tier does not offer: apps sleep after exactly that interval and do not auto-wake — a visitor
lands on a sleep page and must click through it. Phase 5 set the precedent that a divergence gets
written down where the criterion lives, dated and reasoned, rather than planned around silently.

Purpose, second half: 05-08's legibility checkpoint caught three real render defects that every
automated check had passed — a title clipped at both ends, three figures labelled with the wrong
outcome noun, and the corrected label then clipped itself. A test can confirm the selected marker
sits at the artifact's own (k, value). Only a reader can confirm the figure is still legible as that
marker moves, with a sixth legend entry now present, at the app's rendered width of roughly 730 CSS
px where the legend's 7.5 pt entries display near 9.5 CSS px.

Output: two amended ROADMAP criteria, a recorded human approval, and any remedy that approval
required.
</objective>
