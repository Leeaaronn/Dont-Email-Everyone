<objective>
Add `uplift_at_k` and `tie_diagnostics` to `dont_email_everyone/evaluation.py`, and pin the exact
algebraic bridge between uplift-at-k and the Qini curve.

Purpose: PITFALLS.md Pitfall 8 names the conflation of "uplift *within* the top-k" with "cumulative
incremental response *per head of total population*" as a constant source of wrong numbers — the two
differ by a factor of k. RESEARCH.md discovered and verified (to 1.4e-17) the exact conversion
between them, so this plan turns a documented warning into a structural impossibility. D-04's
`tie_diagnostics` lands here because Phase 4 needs to state a tie fraction rather than hand-wave when
a learner's ranking looks coarse.

Output: two new public functions in `evaluation.py`, and their test section in
`tests/test_evaluation.py`.
</objective>

The identity to pin (verified to 1.4e-17), where n_k = int(n * k):
    uplift_at_k(score, t, y, k)  ==  qini[n_k] * N_t / n_t(k)
with N_t the total treated count and n_t(k) the *realized* treated count inside the top-k.
The near-miss that is the trap:  qini[n_k] / k  — 0.09429 vs the correct 0.09384 at k=0.20.

  - **The uplift-at-k convention, `'overall'`.** Take the top-k of the *combined* sample, then
    difference the treated and control mean outcomes *within that selection*. Not top-k within each
    arm separately, which is `'by_group'`. State the reason, not just the choice: `'overall'` is the
    quantity the deployment decision actually produces — you rank the whole list once, mail the top
    k, and observe what the mailed-versus-not comparison inside that slice yields. `'by_group'`
    describes an experiment nobody runs, because in deployment there is no separate control ranking
    to take a top-k of. State that the selection size is `int(n * k)` — **truncation, not rounding**
    — matching the reference implementation, and that the rule is stated because two call sites
    disagreeing on it is the failure mode.
  - **Units, and the exact bridge to the curve.** `uplift_at_k` is an average incremental outcome
    **per targeted customer**; `Q(phi)` is an average incremental outcome **per treated customer in
    the whole population**. They are not the same quantity and differ by roughly a factor of k. The
    exact conversion, verified to 1.4e-17, is `uplift_at_k(k) == Q(k) * N_t / n_t(k)`, dividing by
    the **realized** treated count inside the top-k. It is **not** `Q(k) / k`: measured on the real
    mens frame at k=0.20 the two give 0.09384 and 0.09429, a 0.5% gap that is invisible to anyone
    eyeballing them, because `n_t(k)` fluctuates around `k * N_t` rather than equalling it.
    The following substrings MUST appear literally in the docstring because Task 2 greps for them:
    `overall`, `by_group`, `per targeted customer`, `truncation`.
  - Add one paragraph to the existing "what this number may not be used for" block recording the
    deliberate trade against scikit-uplift's distinct-value thresholding (see
    `<discretion_resolutions>` above) — boundary-only curve points are tie-invariant but cannot
    answer a continuous top-k question at a 39% largest tie fraction, which the Phase 6 slider needs.

**`uplift_at_k(score, treatment, outcome, k=0.2, *, seed=20260902)`.** Keyword-only after `k` so a
seed can never be passed positionally into `k`. Call `_ranked_arrays` — do **not** write a second

centralization of `cov_type` is the repo precedent for why that is one function and not two.

guard that the slice contains at least one treated and at least one control row, raising

reaches a published business number. Return `float(y_top[t_top == 1].mean() - y_top[t_top == 0].mean())`.
