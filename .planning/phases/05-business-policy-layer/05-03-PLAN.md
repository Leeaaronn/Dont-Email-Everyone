<objective>
Score every holdout row with both arms' primary T-learners, additively, so D-05's
cross-arm optimism measurement becomes computable — and prove that doing so changed
nothing Phase 4 published.

Purpose: `05-RESEARCH.md` measured that the only rows carrying both arms' uplift scores are
the 10,653 control rows. IPW's numerator counts rows whose realized arm matches the
prescribed action; for an argmax policy those are treated rows, and on a womens-arm row the
mens score is NaN. The argmax is undefined on exactly the rows the estimator needs. No
holdout-only sample split repairs this — the winner's curse comes from noise in a single
fitted `û` common to every holdout row. D-15 chose the two-line fix: both `m0` and `m1` are
already in scope inside `train()` and `X` is the full 64,000-row design matrix, so scoring
all 32,001 holdout rows is a `predict` call with no refit and no leakage (the split is one
global 50/50 draw — `model.json.split` confirms it, so every holdout row was unseen by every
fit regardless of arm).

**This plan regenerates a Phase-4 artifact after Phase 4 closed and was independently
verified.** The bit-identity proof in Task 3 is the permission slip. Without it, this is an
unaudited edit to published work.

Output: `scored_holdout.parquet` at `(32001, 43)`, with the 37 original columns proven
identical.
</objective>
