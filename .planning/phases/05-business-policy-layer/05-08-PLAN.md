  <action>
Three functions, each returning a `Figure`, each calling neither `plt.show()` nor `savefig`.
Reuse `_guard_unit`, `_UNIT_SCALE` and `_UNIT_AXIS_LABEL` — a fourth unit vocabulary in this
module is a fourth place the axis label can be wrong.

**`policy_curve_plot(curve, bands, *, contrast, unit, anchor, title=None)`**
  - x axis: targeting depth k, labelled as a percentage of the evaluation frame WITH the
    absolute email count as a secondary annotation or a second axis (D-07: percentage carries
    the meaning, the count carries the reality).
  - y axis: the contrast's own units, per population customer, named explicitly. Do not let a
    reader infer whether this is per targeted or per population customer — `evaluation.py`
    decision (g) calls that conflation PITFALLS Pitfall 8's headline failure mode and all
    three units exist in this phase.
  - Draw the band as a filled ribbon behind the line, never in front.
  - Mark the pre-registered anchor `economics.HEADLINE_CAPACITY` with a labelled vertical
    rule reading as a pre-commitment, not as an optimum.
  - Mark the k = 1 "email everyone" reference point.
  - **Annotate the region where the band spans zero.** Shade it, or hatch it, or draw the zero
    line and label the crossing — pick one and say why in the docstring. This is the honesty
    requirement: on the vs-everyone contrast the interval covers zero at every k, and a figure
    that renders that as a confident line is the figure this phase must not ship.

**`cost_sweep_plot(sweep, *, title=None)`**
  - x axis labelled `cost per email / gross margin (c/m)` — the ratio is the only scalar that
    matters, and labelling it `cost` would invite a reader to adopt a number.
  - y axis: optimal targeting depth k\*, as a percentage.
  - Mark the illustrative (cost, margin) pairs with annotations that read as ASSUMPTIONS, not
    as data. Phase 6 criterion 3 requires the same discipline on screen; establish it here.
  - The step structure must be visible — k\* is a step function of c/m and a smoothed line
    would misrepresent it.

**`optimism_plot(optimism, *, title=None)`**
  - Naive against honest at the anchor, per outcome, with the gap visible as a gap.
  - Every cell carrying the `unproven_` prefix must show that prefix in its label. D-03's
    label travels with the number everywhere it appears, including into a picture.

Each docstring states its inputs' grain, its units, and the one thing a reader could
misread — following `qini_plot`'s precedent of arguing its own choices.
