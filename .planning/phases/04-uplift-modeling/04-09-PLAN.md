Every item below must appear. Sourced from CONTEXT, ROADMAP and 04-RESEARCH.

**Above the results table, in `validity.md`'s `(a)`/`(b)` form:**
- **D-04's ship rule**: a cell ships only if (a) its holdout Qini exceeds the 95th percentile of its own
  permutation null distribution, **and** (b) its uplift ranking beats the response-model baseline on
  the same Qini axes. Both conditions, not either. State that the rule is fixed above every result in
  the document because a decision rule stated after the result it judges is not a decision rule, and
  that it is also stated in `models.py`'s docstring where the code that applies it lives, so the
  criterion and the check cannot drift apart — `validity.md`'s exact framing.
- **D-11's eligibility restriction**: only the six primary linear cells are eligible; both forest
  configurations are diagnostic exhibits and never ship regardless of their Qini. State why: it
  collapses multiplicity from 18 candidate cells to 6 and keeps the pre-registration genuinely prior.
- **D-21's propensity gate**: `|r| > 0.9` against either base-model score fails the cell, and a failing
  cell is reported as a repackaged propensity ranking and cannot ship regardless of its Qini.
- **D-22's calibration gate**: the sign gate is hard; the magnitude band is three times the per-cell
  seed-to-seed standard deviation measured in this repository across 20 split draws, and explicitly not
  an imported relative percentage.
- **D-19's shared-control assumption**: both arms share the same roughly 21,306 control customers, so
  their Qini values cannot be numerically compared — Radcliffe says this explicitly for this dataset —
  and any cross-arm argmax is a winner's-curse estimator over two correlated noisy estimates. Phase 5
  builds and evaluates the policy; Phase 4 supplies the measured incomparability and nothing more.
- **The split-noise caveat**: these numbers come from one pre-committed split. Under a different
  legitimate split draw, mens/visit holdout Qini measured +0.001627 rather than +0.003069 — a roughly
  twofold move. The honest framing is that the ship rule was pre-registered and applied **once** to
  **one** pre-committed split, which is what makes it a valid decision procedure even though the
  underlying estimate is noisy. Say this above the table, not after it.

**In the results sections, each with its italic Source line:**
- The six-cell primary-learner table: train Qini, holdout Qini, ratio, null p95, empirical p-value,
  beats-baseline, both gates, ships. *Source: `data/processed/model_results.parquet` (18 rows).*
- **Conversion and spend as explicitly named negative results** (D-01), with the claim that the
  negatives were tested exactly as hard as the positive (D-02) — same null, same shuffle count, same
  gates, no tiering.
- **The divergence from the project's own research**: PITFALLS Pitfall 4 states conversion uplift is not
  learnable here, holdout Qini zero or negative for every configuration tried. PITFALLS tested the
  **mens** arm only, where this repo reproduces its finding. If womens/conversion cleared the bar in
  this run, that is a genuine divergence from the project's own research and must be written up the way
  03-06 wrote up its divergence, not suppressed. D-01's decision to fit all six cells is what made it
  visible at all.
- **The forest exhibit** (D-10, D-14): the three mens/visit learners' train and holdout Qini and their
  ratios, against PITFALLS' cited 240x / 32x / 1.3x. If the default forest reproduced the cited ratio
  closely, the report may say *reproduced* rather than *cited* — a stronger sentence than 02-06 could
  make about its coverage figure. **But state explicitly that the ratio is one number from one split
  and is not a stable property**: under the alternative split draw the same configuration gave a
  spectacular train Qini against a *negative* holdout Qini.
- **D-16's replicate-count justification**: the p95 estimated from the first 50 draws against all 200 of
  the same stream, showing up to a 19.8% shift on womens/visit — the cell that ships. This converts
  D-16 from an assertion into a demonstrated decision, the direct analogue of 02-04's coverage-band
  widening.
- **The two nulls named distinctly** (04-RESEARCH Pitfall 4): one sentence each. The permutation null
  permutes the treatment label within the training half and refits both base models; `qini_random_band`
  shuffles the score and refits nothing. A reader who meets both without this will conflate them.
- **The tie diagnostics**, stated rather than hand-waved (03-CONTEXT D-04 asked for exactly this): the
  tie counts are identical across all three outcomes for a given arm because ties are a property of
  duplicate rows in the design matrix, not of the learner — roughly 21,307 mens holdout rows over only
  18,922 distinct feature vectors, so about 11-12% of customers share a feature vector and are tied
  under any deterministic model. Note that this retroactively justifies Phase 3's seeded tie-break.
- **The calibration and propensity diagnostics** as explicitly passed or failed checks, with the
  measured maximum `|r|` and which cell carries it.
- **The D-20 cross-arm block**: per-arm mean, spread, minimum and negative fraction; the correlation on
  the shared control holdout; the sign-disagreement fraction. **State the STATE.md blocker's empirical
  answer and the arm swap explicitly**: the blocker asked whether a genuine negative-uplift segment
  survives holdout validation on the mens arm, and the measured answer is no on mens and yes on womens.
- **The encoding-convention note**: all-K one-hot is the third convention in this repo, chosen for
  stated reasons, cross-referencing `balance.py`'s decision (b) — 04-RESEARCH §Q2 recommends the report
  repeat it.
- **The hurdle-model note** (D-03): a one-line statement naming the two-part hurdle model as the
  principled alternative for a zero-inflated outcome, rejected here because it would break the one-shape
  T-learner and because its first stage is exactly the conversion cell already being fit.
- **The figure curation rule** (plan 04-08): why the committed set is a curated subset rather than one
  figure per cell, so the absent figures read as a decision. Reference each committed figure by filename.
- **The convention note**: one line stating that this report traces numbers to committed artifacts,
  unlike `metric.md` which cites pytest node IDs, because Phase 3 persisted nothing and Phase 4
  persists four artifacts.
