<critical_no_fabrication>
**ROADMAP Phase 2 success criterion #2 and CONTEXT.md D-04 both describe a "stray significant per-covariate p-value." No such covariate exists in this data.** All 21 p-values are >= 0.19377.

Under H-null with 21 tests, P(zero significant) = 0.95^21 ~= 34%, so getting zero is unremarkable — it simply is not what the roadmap sentence assumed.

Consequences for this plan, which are non-negotiable:
- No test may assert `(p < 0.05).sum() == 1` or any variant that presumes a significant covariate exists.
- No docstring or comment may claim "one covariate was significant."
- The correct framing is a **pre-registered decision rule**: randomization is accepted if (a) every abs(SMD) < 0.1 across all three comparisons and (b) the omnibus LR test does not reject at alpha = 0.05. Per-covariate p-values are reported for completeness and are explicitly NOT part of the acceptance criterion, because with 21 tests roughly one p < 0.05 is expected under perfect randomization.
- `balance.py`'s module docstring must state that decision rule. Plan 02-06 carries the same rule into `reports/validity.md` prose.
</critical_no_fabrication>
