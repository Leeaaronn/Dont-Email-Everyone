<critical_do_not_assert_equality>
**CONTEXT.md D-08 and the phase Specific Ideas both frame PITFALLS.md's coverage percentages (96.5 / 95.2 / 92.8 / 84.5) as a cross-check. Do NOT turn that into an equality assertion.**

A faithful reimplementation gives 0.9467 at n=4,000 — a 1.8pp gap against a Monte-Carlo standard error of 0.0034 at R=4,000, which is about five standard errors. The gap is real, not noise: PITFALLS.md does not state its DGP, replicate count, or seed, and coverage simulations are sensitive to whether the population is the empirical spend vector, a fitted parametric model, both arms pooled, or a shift-injected control, and to whether cell size means total or per-arm. A parametric zero-inflated-lognormal DGP calibrated to the same rates gives 0.7963 / 0.8960 / 0.9350 / 0.9480 — further still.

`assert abs(coverage - 0.965) < 0.005` **will fail.**

Assert instead on properties any correct implementation must satisfy: monotone degradation as cell size shrinks, coverage at or above 0.94 at the full cell size, coverage at or below 0.90 at 400. Use the **median CI widths** as the tight numeric cross-check against PITFALLS.md — those match within 3% at every cell size. Document the discrepancy in the module docstring so a future agent does not "fix" a non-bug.
</critical_do_not_assert_equality>
